---
title: Grow a box's disk
surface: termag
category: new
date: 2026-11-03
---
Resize box in a session's ⋯ menu now has a New disk beside the size, up to 300 GB. Change either or both at once; growing the disk keeps the box running, and a disk can grow once every six hours. `termag box resize <box> --disk <GB>` does the same from the command line.
